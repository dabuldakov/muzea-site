Delete your muzea account

The «muzea» app · operator: Dmitry Aleksandrovich Buldakov

This page explains how to delete your «muzea» account. You can delete the account in the app or by email if you cannot access the app.

1. Delete in the app

  1. Open the «muzea» app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

After confirmation the account is hidden immediately: you cannot sign in, sessions and push tokens are revoked, and the profile disappears from contacts and search. You can restore the account through support within 30 days. After 30 days the data is permanently deleted.

2. Delete by email

If you cannot access the app, send an email from the address registered to the account to dabuldakov@mail.ru.

  • Subject: “Delete muzea account”.
  • Include the username and email address linked to the account.

We may ask you to confirm that the email was sent by the account owner. The request is processed within 10 business days after confirmation.

3. What happens immediately

  • the account is marked for deletion and sign-in is disabled;
  • sessions, refresh tokens and device push tokens are revoked;
  • the profile is hidden from contacts, search and groups;
  • name, surname, username and avatar are not shown to other users.

4. What is deleted after 30 days

  • the account and profile data: name, surname, username, email, phone, avatar;
  • contacts and the blocked users list;
  • messages, attachments, images, videos and voice messages you sent;
  • news, posts, videos and comments you created;
  • likes and reactions linked to your account.

In chats, deleted messages are replaced with technical “Message deleted” markers without text, attachments or author. Recipients may keep local copies on their devices; the server does not restore them. Backups are overwritten within 7 days, and security logs are retained for up to 90 days.

5. Restoring your account

Within 30 days after deletion you can restore the account: email dabuldakov@mail.ru from the registered address. After identity verification, access is restored, but you must accept the personal data processing consent again. After 30 days restoration is impossible; you need to register again.

6. Timing

The account is hidden immediately. After 30 days the data is permanently deleted. For email requests, the request is processed within 10 business days after confirmation. Backups are overwritten within 7 days, and security logs are retained for up to 90 days.

7. Contacts

For questions about account deletion and personal data: dabuldakov@mail.ru, +7 913 800-24-16.
