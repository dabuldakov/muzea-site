Delete your muzea account

The «muzea» app · operator: Dmitry Aleksandrovich Buldakov

This page explains how to delete your «muzea» account. You can delete the account in the app or by email if you cannot access the app.

1. Delete in the app

  1. Open the «muzea» app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

After confirmation the account is deactivated immediately: you can no longer sign in, active sessions end, and your name, surname and username are hidden from other users. Messages, posts, videos and other data are retained — your conversations remain with the other participants but become read-only.

2. Delete by email

If you cannot access the app, send an email from the address registered to the account to dabuldakov@mail.ru.

  • Subject: “Delete muzea account”.
  • Include the username and email address linked to the account.

We may ask you to confirm that the email was sent by the account owner. The request is processed within 10 business days after confirmation.

3. What happens to the account

  • sign-in is disabled and active sessions and device tokens are revoked;
  • your name, surname and username are hidden throughout the app and replaced with “Deleted user”;
  • private chats with you remain, but no new messages can be sent to them;
  • you are removed from groups; your group messages remain;
  • the push notification token is disabled.

4. What data is retained

The account is not erased permanently: the data stays on the servers, but access to it is closed.

  • the account and profile data (name, surname, username, email, avatar);
  • messages, attachments and GIFs you sent in chats;
  • contacts and the blocked users list;
  • news, posts and videos you created;
  • database backups are kept for no more than 7 days and are then overwritten;
  • technical logs and records required by law are retained for the period established by law.

5. Restoring your account

As long as the data is retained, the account can be restored. Email dabuldakov@mail.ru from the registered address; after we verify your identity, access and your display name are enabled again, and your conversations and posts remain in place.

6. Timing

When you delete the account in the app, it is deactivated immediately. For email requests, this happens within 10 business days after the request is confirmed. Backups are overwritten within 7 days.

7. Contacts

For questions about deleting or restoring an account and personal data: dabuldakov@mail.ru, +7 913 800-24-16.
