Operator — a person who, alone or jointly with others, organizes and/or carries out the processing of personal data. Processing — any action with personal data performed with or without the use of automation tools. User — a natural person using the Application.

4.1. Access to device data

The Application does not request access to the entire photo library and does not read the device address book. Photos, videos and files are selected through the built-in Android (Photo Picker) and iOS (Photos Picker) system mechanisms: the app only receives the files the User explicitly chooses.

  • camera and microphone access — only while recording a video note or a voice message;
  • notifications — optional; they can be disabled in settings;
  • saving to the gallery — a separate permission requested only when the User taps “Save”;
  • contacts in the Application are muzea users added by username, not entries from the phone's address book.

Permissions can be revoked in Android or iOS settings; the rest of the Application keeps working.

5. Purposes of processing

  • registration, authentication and account management;
  • messaging, operation of contacts and group chats;
  • sending push notifications about new messages and events;
  • displaying the User's profile and status to other participants;
  • posting and displaying posts and videos;
  • ensuring security, preventing abuse, handling requests;
  • compliance with the requirements of the legislation of the Russian Federation.

6. Legal grounds

Processing is carried out on the basis of the User's consent given at the first launch of the Application and upon registration, as well as for the purposes of performing the agreement to which the User is a party and for performing the duties imposed on the Operator by law.

7. Procedure and conditions of processing

Processing includes collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), extraction, use, transfer (provision of access), depersonalization, blocking, deletion, destruction and restriction of access.

Processing of Users' personal data is carried out:

  • on the Application servers (the main server and the chat module server) located in the territory of the Russian Federation;
  • on the User's device — storage of authorization tokens and caching of downloaded content. Credentials are excluded from the device's backup systems (auto-backup is disabled).

Processing by third parties is not carried out, except in cases expressly specified in section 8 of this Policy.

8. Cross-border transfer

Certain data is transferred outside the Russian Federation:

  • Google Ireland Limited (Ireland) and Google LLC (USA) — FCM: device push token, notification type, identifiers, and, if included in the push, the notification text, sender name and chat title;
  • Apple Inc. (USA) — APNs for push notifications in the iOS app;
  • KLIPY (USA) — the GIF search query and the device IP address;
  • third-party websites — when building link previews, the device contacts the website from the message and sends its IP address;
  • recipients' email providers — if a sign-in code email is sent to Gmail, iCloud or another foreign mailbox.

Sign-in code emails are sent via Mail.ru (Russia). The Operator notifies the authorized body of its intention to carry out cross-border transfer in accordance with the established procedure.

10. Security measures

  • identification and authentication of Users, differentiation of access rights;
  • encryption of the data transmission channel between the Application and the server (HTTPS/TLS);
  • storage of one-time codes and session tokens only as irreversible hashes;
  • placement of personal data on servers located in the territory of the Russian Federation;
  • exclusion of credentials from device backups;
  • encryption of backups on the Operator's media, restriction of physical access to server equipment;
  • accounting of personal data media, internal control and appointment of a person responsible for organizing the processing of personal data.

  • to withdraw consent to the processing of personal data at any time;
  • to delete the account;
  • to appeal the Operator's actions to Roskomnadzor (rkn.gov.ru) or to a court.

The User is obliged to provide accurate information during registration and to keep it up to date in the Application profile.

12. Withdrawal of consent and account deletion

  1. 12.1. Consent may be withdrawn in the Application under “Profile” (the “Delete account” function) or by email to dabuldakov@mail.ru.
  2. 12.2. The Operator stops processing and blocks the User's personal data for 30 days after receiving the withdrawal or account deletion. During this period the account can be restored through support; restoration requires a new consent. After 30 days the data is destroyed, unless a different period is required by law.
  3. 12.3. When the account is deleted, sessions and device tokens are revoked, and the profile, contacts, push tokens, messages, attachments, posts and videos are deleted from the server. Chats keep technical markers without text, attachments or author. The User is removed from groups; chat ownership is transferred to the next participant.
  4. 12.4. Within 30 days the account can be restored through support. After 30 days restoration is impossible; to return, the User must register again, and previous messages and posts are not restored.
  5. 12.5. User requests are processed within 10 business days of receipt, unless a different period is required by law.
